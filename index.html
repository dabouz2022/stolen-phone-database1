Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find my IMEI number?

You can find your IMEI by dialing *#06# on your phone, or go to Settings > About Phone > IMEI. You can also find it on the original box or receipt.

Is my personal information secure?

Yes, we use encryption to protect all your data. Your personal information is only shared when someone finds your phone and needs to contact you.

What should I do if I find a stolen phone?

Use our "Check Phone" feature to search for the IMEI number. If it's in our database, you'll see the owner's contact information to arrange return.

How long does my report stay in the database?

Your report stays active indefinitely until you mark it as recovered or contact us to remove it. You can also update your report anytime.

Can I report a phone that was lost, not stolen?

Yes, you can report any missing phone regardless of how it was lost. This helps honest people who find it to return it to you.

Report to Algerian Police

If your phone has been stolen, you can also report it to the Algerian police through their official online portal. This will help in the official investigation and recovery process.
